WELCOME TO ORBIT

A little room to make a big mix.

  1. Load your music. Try A, B, C or D next to a demo, or import files from your device.
  2. Prepare your set. Create a crate in the library. Select songs in All tracks and use Add selected, then use ↑ / ↓ in the crate to arrange the order. Backup crates saves its list, without audio.
  3. Find your blend. Play the decks, shape them with EQ and choose LEFT / RIGHT / THRU for each channel. Move the crossfader to blend its two sides.
  4. Make it yours. Click a cue pad to save a point. Click again to jump. Shift-click to clear.
  5. Keep a moment going. Choose 1, 2, 4, 8 or 16 beats to loop from the current beat. Click again to exit.
  6. Shape the room. Use ECHO and ROOM under each deck. Echo follows the track BPM and tempo; RESET FX clears both effects.
  7. Trigger a sample. Choose a pad, a collection track and a 0.05–30 second region in the Sampler panel. Load it, then click the pad or press Z/X/C/V. Shift-click clears a pad.
  8. Save the set. Record mix captures the master output. Stop to get a downloadable recording.

Beat sync matches tempo and beat phase once, using the grid you set. Choose a SYNC LEADER and play it first: sync starts this deck if paused. Repeat after tempo or position changes. SET BEAT 1 defines the grid anchor; − / ＋ move it by 10 ms. Q snaps newly placed cue points to the nearest beat; it does not schedule cue playback. KEY preserves pitch during speed changes. Pause the deck before switching KEY; playback with KEY prepares about 90 ms of audio before starting. Without KEY, speed changes also change pitch. Imported BPM values are estimates: edit the BPM or use TAP. Cue points and imported files are saved in this browser when local storage is available. PFL headphone preview is available with split output in Audio settings. MIDI Learn is available in the MIDI panel. Stems and streaming are planned.

Keys: 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 play or pause decks A / B / C / D. Space pauses all decks and samples. Audio format support depends on your browser. Keep this tab active for the most reliable playback.

AUDIO OUTPUT

Hear the next track first.

Split mode needs a DJ splitter cable: connect the LEFT mono output to headphones and the RIGHT mono output to speakers. Select PFL on a deck to preview it independently of channel volume, master volume and crossfader. Both selected decks are summed in the headphones.

Normal stereo mode keeps PFL out of the speakers. Recordings always capture the stereo master, never the headphone preview. Dedicated multi-channel audio interfaces and ASIO are not supported yet.
